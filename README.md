# 🌍 EcoPath AI

> Personalized AI-powered climate action planner using Google Gemini.

## 📖 About

EcoPath AI is a web application that helps users understand and reduce their environmental impact through AI-powered insights and personalized action plans. By combining climate education with Google Gemini AI, users receive recommendations tailored to their daily lifestyle.

---

## ❗ Problem

Many people care about climate change but don't know:

- How their lifestyle affects the environment
- Which actions make the biggest difference
- How to build sustainable habits

---

## 💡 Solution

EcoPath AI analyzes users' daily habits and provides personalized recommendations to help them make more sustainable choices.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🌍 Climate Change Information & News
Stay informed with the latest climate change news, environmental articles, and educational content presented in an easy-to-understand format.

### 🤖 AI Climate Lifestyle Analysis
Users complete a short lifestyle assessment, and Google Gemini analyzes their habits to identify their biggest environmental impacts and provide personalized sustainability insights.

### 📅 AI Lifestyle Planner
Based on the AI analysis, Gemini generates a realistic weekly action plan with simple, achievable goals to help users gradually build eco-friendly habits.

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, JavaScript
- **Backend:** Python, Flask
- **AI:** Google Gemini API
- **Database:** SQLite

---

## ⚙️ How It Works

User --> Lifestyle Assessment --> Google Gemini AI --> Personal Climate Analysis --> AI Lifestyle Plan

---

## 🌎 Impact

EcoPath AI helps users:

- Learn about climate change
- Understand their environmental impact
- Build sustainable daily habits
- Contribute to climate change mitigation
